## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an elevated tropical hospitality ethos tailored for the high-end Sri Lankan leisure and day-pass resort sector. The target audience encompasses affluent domestic vacationers, regional South Asian short-stay travelers, and discerning international tourists seeking verified luxury day villas, coastal cabanas, tea-estate retreats, and private nature sanctuaries.

The aesthetic philosophy fuses **Modern Tropical Luxury** with architectural clarity. It avoids clichéd colonial nostalgia, opting instead for contemporary Geoffrey Bawa-inspired tropical modernism: expansive breathable white planes, grounded slate typography, deeply saturated biological greens, and luminous golden-hour terracotta highlights.

Key emotional anchors:
- **Sanctuary and Respite:** Effortless, calm whitespace reminiscent of open-air verandas and ocean breezes.
- **Absolute Assurance:** Crisp, deliberate data visualization, unambiguous real-time reservation timers, verified property badges, and frictionless dual-currency/trilingual clarity.
- **Sensory Indulgence:** Editorial composition paired with tactile micro-interactions that feel warm, curated, and exclusive.

## Colors

The palette balances lush botanical density with sun-baked architectural minerals and cool oceanic tides:

- **Primary (`#0D4D3D` - Ceylon Emerald):** Evokes the deep canopy of Sinharaja and high-grown tea estates. Anchor for primary conversion flows, top-level navigation, and master brand markers.
- **Secondary (`#197A76` - Indian Ocean Teal):** Represents coastal reefs and clear lagoon waters. Governs active tabs, search filtration states, verified host iconography, and interactive card indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#D97736` - Terracotta Sun):** Warm, luminous, and directional. Reserved for high-urgency inventory holds, flash booking perks, verified partner star distinctions, and critical reservation countdowns.
- **Neutral (`#1E292A` - Polished Slate):** A charcoal-slate with balanced blue-green undertones that prevents harsh contrasts while delivering crisp, accessible readability against bright, sunlit white surfaces.
- **Surface & Backgrounds:** The base canvas lives on pristine warm-milk tint (`#FAFAF7`), while cards and elevation planes rest on sharp pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with whisper-thin jade-slate borders (`rgba(13, 77, 61, 0.08)`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy delivers architectural discipline balanced by welcoming warmth:

- **Display & Headlines (`Epilogue`):** Delivers a sculptural, editorial character. Its geometric yet organic contours echo tropical modernist signage and high-end boutique hospitality branding.
- **Body & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Ensures effortless legibility across dense booking specifications, meal inclusion lists, and reservation terms. Generous counters maintain readability even under high glare and mobile outdoor use.
- **Utility, Currencies & Countdowns (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated to technical data nodes: reservation hold timers, LKR/USD exchange tickers, check-in time windows, and booking reference IDs. This deliberate contrast prevents visual ambiguity in critical transactions.
- **Multilingual Support (EN / SI / TA):** Sinhala and Tamil renderings inherit vertical line-height multipliers of `1.2x` on top of standard base tokens to accommodate ascenders, descenders, and complex conjuncts without clipping.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system employs a disciplined 12-column fluid grid system across desktop viewports, shifting to a 6-column grid on tablet and a 4-column grid on mobile devices.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 640px` (4 columns, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`)
  - Tablet: `640px – 1024px` (6 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`)
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (12 columns, max-content container: `1320px`, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`)

- **Rhythm & Safe Margins:**
  Layouts prioritize vertical breathing room between discovery modules. Booking flows employ sticky checkout rails on desktop (`col-span-4`) locked next to experiential media galleries (`col-span-8`). On mobile screens, critical booking progress, dynamic currency switches, and inventory hold timers lock to an accessible sticky bottom bar with safe-area padding (`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons heavy artificial drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layers** and **Tinted Ambient Illumination**:

- **Ground Level (Base Canvas):** `#FAFAF7` provides an organic, glare-free backdrop.
- **Layer 1 (Cards, Property Grids, Search Bars):** Solid `#FFFFFF` elevated via a crisp, hairline low-contrast outline (`1px solid rgba(13, 77, 61, 0.08)`), paired with an ultra-diffuse ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(13, 77, 61, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Booking Drawers, Filter Overlays, Popovers):** Solid `#FFFFFF` lifted with `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(30, 41, 42, 0.08)`, rimmed with a slightly firmer boundary (`1px solid rgba(13, 77, 61, 0.12)`).
- **Layer 3 (Modals, Gallery Lightboxes, Fullscreen Checkout Locks):** Backdrop curtain composed of high-density ocean slate tint (`rgba(14, 26, 25, 0.65)`) with a `8px` progressive backdrop blur, focusing absolute attention onto the interaction center.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a curated architectural curvature (`roundedness: 2`). This avoids clinical sharp edges while remaining firmly grounded, avoiding overly playful or child-like pill forms for structural surfaces:

- **Base Components (Inputs, Primary Buttons, Dropdowns):** `0.5rem` (`8px`) corner radius.
- **Containers & Feature Cards (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (`16px`) corner radius.
- **Large Modals, Hero Image Frames, Experience Hubs (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (`24px`) corner radius.
- **Exceptions (Badges, Currency Toggles, Pill Indicators):** Fully circular or pill-shaped (`9999px`) purely for micro-status tokens (e.g., "Verified Sanctuary", "Hold: 09:42", "EN | SI | TA").

## Components

### Buttons & Booking Actions
- **Primary Action (Book Now / Hold Experience):** Solid Emerald (`#0D4D3D`) base, white text, `0.5rem` radius, subtle hover scale (`scale-[1.01]`) accompanied by an increase in deep green ambient radiance (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(13, 77, 61, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary Action (Explore Packages / View Menu):** Crisp white surface with a `1.5px` border in `#197A76`, typography set in `#197A76`, shifting to faint teal mist on hover (`rgba(25, 122, 118, 0.06)`).
- **Urgent Inventory Action:** Highlighted with `#D97736` background and embedded monospace countdown timer.

### Badges & Status Tokens
- **Verification Seal:** Emerald tint background (`rgba(13, 77, 61, 0.08)`), Emerald text (`#0D4D3D`), accompanied by a verified check icon.
- **Multilingual Switcher:** Compact segmented chip toggling between `EN`, `සිං`, and `தம`, displaying active state with a solid slate pill and white glyph.
- **Currency Switcher & Real-Time Converter:** Inline floating badge displaying `LKR` and `USD` side-by-side with instantaneous exchange rate parity hints (`1 USD ≈ 305 LKR`) in `JetBrains Mono`.

### Real-Time Inventory Hold Countdown
- An amber-terracotta status strip (`#D97736` background with high-contrast white text) that locks to the top of checkout interfaces. Utilizes `JetBrains Mono` for a live ticking countdown (`09:59 MINS REMAINING`), conveying urgency without jarring visual noise.

### Cards & Property Teasers
- Pure white container, `rounded-lg` (`16px`), zero border shadow replaced with a micro-stroke (`rgba(13, 77, 61, 0.08)`).
- Visual gallery capped with a 16:10 aspect ratio, overlaying a gradient curtain at the top for multilingual tag pills and day-pass hours (`09:00 AM – 06:00 PM`).
- Price footer split cleanly: bold headline price in `LKR`, paired with a secondary `USD` equivalent in neutral slate.

### Inputs & Date-Guest Controls
- Custom split selector for: (1) Date Selection, (2) Pass Duration (Full Day / Morning / Sunset Pool Pass), and (3) Guest Counter (Adults, Children).
- Input field boundaries default to `1px solid rgba(30, 41, 42, 0.15)`. Active/focused states transition smoothly to `2px solid #0D4D3D` with zero outline rings.
