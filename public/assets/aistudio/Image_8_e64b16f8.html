The Sanctuary Experience

Day Passes & Stays

Perched directly on the golden sandy shores of Bentota, The Palms Oceanfront Sanctuary pairs coastal serenity with refined Sri Lankan tropical hospitality. Built around lush coconut groves and a pristine 40-meter tiered saltwater infinity pool, guests enjoy breezy open-air verandas, private shaded day cabanas, and culinary journeys ranging from live lagoon-crab grills to spiced Ceylon afternoon teas.
